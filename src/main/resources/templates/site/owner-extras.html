    Haggle mode 🪙

    Let customers bargain with your shop's AI, like at a market stall. It talks in their language and gives way slowly. Your lowest price is enforced by BazaarReady's code, not by the AI, so no one can talk it lower. An agreed price is saved in their cart for 30 minutes.

    Saved.

    Haggle mode works on shop sites, where people add things to a cart.

    Add prices to your products first; haggling needs a price to start from.
