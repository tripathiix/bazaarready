How BazaarReady works

BazaarReady is a launch agent for small businesses and creators. You describe your business in your own words, and it builds a real website, tests it with AI customers, keeps you safe from fake collabs, and keeps improving it when you ask.

No forms to fill, no design skills needed. Just talk.

Getting started, in six steps

  1. Pick your language

    Eight languages: English, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali and Gujarati. Your website can be in any language.

  2. Describe your business

    Type it, press "Speak instead" and say it, or upload exported WhatsApp chats with your customers. Not sure about your idea yet? Open the Idea coach first.

  3. Answer a few questions

    If something important is missing, like prices or your area, the agent asks. Usually two to four short questions.

  4. Look at the preview and test it

    Open the preview, and press "Test with AI customers" to see where real people might get stuck.

  5. Publish

    Say yes and your website is live with its own link to share on WhatsApp, Instagram or anywhere.

  6. Run it from your private dashboard

    See orders and messages, check collab offers, change your website and add your own photos. Save that link.

Tips for the best website

  1. List everything you sell or offer, with a price for each

    "Chocolate cake ₹900 for 1 kg" works far better than "I make cakes".

  2. Say what your business is about and who it's for

    "Home bakery for birthdays and small parties in Pune."

  3. Say where you work or deliver, and your timings

    Customers decide quickly when they know you can reach them.

  4. Add your rules

    Delivery charges, how far ahead to order, cancellations and refunds. The AI customers will tell you if anything is missing.

  5. Upload your own photos

    Your real products always beat stock photos.

Questions

Do I need to know anything about websites?

No. You only talk or type about your business. BazaarReady does the rest.

Can I change my website later?

Yes, at any time. Open your dashboard, choose "Change my website" and say what you want changed.

What happens to my WhatsApp chats?

They are read once to understand your business. Customers' names and phone numbers are removed before anything is saved, and the files themselves are not kept.

Are the AI customers' orders real?

No. They are clearly marked tests and are removed automatically when testing ends, so you only see real orders.

Is the collab check a guarantee?

No. It is a judgement from public facts, like how old the sender's website is and whether their email is properly set up. One rule always holds: never pay a fee to get a collab.

I lost my dashboard link. What now?

Keep it safe and bookmarked. For now, a lost link can't be recovered on your own, so save it as soon as your site is ready.
