Tips for the best website

  1. List everything you sell or offer, with a price for each“Chocolate cake ₹900 for 1 kg” works far better than “I make cakes”.
  2. Say what your business is about and who it's for“Home bakery for birthdays and small parties in Pune.”
  3. Say where you work or deliver, and your timingsCustomers decide quickly when they know you can reach them.
  4. Add your rulesDelivery charges, how far ahead to order, cancellations and refunds. The AI customers will tell you if anything is missing.
  5. Upload your own photosYour real products always beat stock photos.

Questions

Do I need to know anything about websites?

No. You only talk or type about your business. BazaarReady does the rest.

Can I change my website later?

Yes, at any time. Open your dashboard, choose “Change my website” and say what you want changed.

What happens to my WhatsApp or Instagram chats?

They are read once to understand your business. Customers' names and phone numbers are removed before anything is saved, and the files themselves are not kept.

Are the AI customers' orders real?

No. They are clearly marked tests and are removed automatically when testing ends, so you only see real orders.

Is the collab check a guarantee?

No. It is a judgement from public facts, like how old the sender's website is and whether their email is properly set up. One rule always holds: never pay a fee to get a collab.
