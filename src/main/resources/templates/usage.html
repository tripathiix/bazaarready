How we keep it efficient

Prompt caching

The fixed instructions and tools are cached, and multi-step work re-reads the earlier steps from the cache instead of paying for them again.

The right model for the job

Claude Sonnet 5.5 builds websites and plays test customers. The much cheaper Claude Haiku 5.5 handles quick jobs like checking photos and translating labels.

Daily limits

A cap on AI calls per day, for the whole service and for each account, so the credits can't be used up by mistake or abuse.
